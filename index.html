Idea to Prototype Pipeline

Result

One-line idea to clickable prototype in 1–3 minutes, reviewed by 7 people across PM, design and engineering.

What it does

Turns a one-line idea into a structured spec covering the problem, target user, screens, and what's explicitly out of scope. It includes one editable review checkpoint, then Claude generates the prototype code directly, rendered live inside the app with Sandpack, no hosted preview dependency.

Why I built it

As a PM, turning a rough idea into something a stakeholder or engineer can respond to normally means writing a spec, then waiting on design or engineering time to see it as a real screen. I wanted to test whether AI could compress that loop into minutes, without needing to pull anyone else in first.

AI Decision Navigator

Result

Across 10 real decisions with 7 people, it never made the decision itself.

What it does

Surfaces the assumptions, constraints and options in a high-stakes decision while leaving the final call to the user.

Why I built it

High-stakes decisions rarely have one right answer, and AI tools tend to jump to one anyway. I wanted to see whether prompt-layer guardrails could keep a model structuring the thinking without making the call. I closed 4 reasoning failures with guardrails and evaluated the tool on 5 qualitative criteria instead of accuracy, since no single answer was correct.
